Introduction
You must guide a millipede around collecting hearts and avoiding obstacles.

This is a snake like game where the millipede is constantly moving and you control which direction it should turn. As you collect hearts, your millipede will grow and score points. There are 10 difficulties that increase the number of obstacles to avoid. Higher difficulties also reward you with more points per heart. Every 10th heart you collect, may also add a single obstacle to the play area. Good luck!

Controls
On the title screen use up (W) and down (S) to change the difficulty, and (space) to begin.
Use the keyboard (WASD) to change the direction of the millipede.

I've also included a trainer that can be accessed from the title screen by pressing (T).
